Welcome to Genmai

Genmai is a Japanese input method for macOS. It converts what you type into kana and kanji right on your Mac, and learns the words you use.

  • You can install Genmai for every user of this Mac (/Library/Input Methods, which needs an administrator) or only for you (~/Library/Input Methods, no administrator needed). You can uninstall it from Genmai’s settings (General).
  • AI conversion is optional and off by default. Turn it on in the welcome window that opens after the installation, or in the AI section of Genmai’s settings (it downloads the AI model). Conversion still happens entirely on your Mac.
  • After the installation, log out and log back in: Genmai then appears in the input menu (no restart needed).
