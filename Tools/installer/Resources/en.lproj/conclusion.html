Genmai is ready

Genmai has been added to your input sources. To type Japanese:

  1. Click the input menu in the menu bar and choose Hiragana (Genmai). You can also press Control-Space or the かな key to switch.
  2. If Genmai isn’t in the menu, open System Settings › Keyboard, click Edit… next to Input Sources, click +, choose Japanese and add Genmai.

To change how Genmai converts or to turn on AI conversion, select Genmai and open its settings from the input menu.
